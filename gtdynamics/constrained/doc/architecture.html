03 / From ambient costs to a step

Linearization and the LM trial

B maps reduced step coordinates ξ to an ambient increment Δx = Bξ that satisfies the linearized equalities. Cost factors can couple components and free variables, so the reduced problem remains a sparse global solve.

Tangent space

The ambient increments that satisfy the equalities to first order: Dh(x)Δx = 0. Dh is the equality Jacobian. Columns of B span these directions; ξ specifies an increment in that basis.

Retraction

The update that maps a local increment back to a point satisfying the nonlinear constraints, within the chosen policy’s tolerance. A tangent step alone generally leaves a curved equality surface.

Tangent cone

The tangent directions that also satisfy Dgactive(x)Δx ≤ 0. Dg is an inequality Jacobian. At a boundary these directions need not include both signs of every tangent vector.

Automatic discovery

Ambient constraint factors → connected components → runtime types + initial points. Requested initial projection runs before the first numerical basis.

Explicit construction

Supply typed points, bindings, and free values. Both entry paths feed the same optimizer machinery for that family.

Active inequality: constrain the local step

Inactive inequality: enforce during retraction

At x > 0 the inequality is strict, so its row is omitted from the QP. A large step can still cross the boundary. Nonlinear retraction enforces the full constraint using the selected policy and tolerances.

05 / Structure versus numerical geometry

Fixing kinematics changes
the equality basis

Conditioning means fixing selected variables and treating their values as parameters. Fixing different joint configurations q and velocities v changes the dynamics coefficients, even when the remaining equations are affine—linear plus a constant.

Shared definition

Robot + constraint factors

Local layout, physical equations, and policy. Fixed quantities are immutable conditioning parameters, not mutable state in a shared type.

→
Condition on qk, vkSlice k

New type, free layout, and equality preparation. Affine mechanics in acceleration, wrench, and torque can use a read-only cached Bk under the explicit affine contract.

Condition on qk+1, vk+1Slice k+1

A distinct conditioned type and cache. Bk+1 need not equal Bk; affinity is never inferred just because the structures match.

During articulated nonlinear retraction, geometry conditioned on recovered trial q/v is temporary and trial-specific. It must not overwrite an invariant cache belonging to another conditioned type.

Structural preparation and numerical basis construction

Type-owned preparation selects layout, independent coordinates, and symbolic ordering. Nonlinear equalities need a fresh numerical B at each point. For explicitly affine equalities Ax = b with fixed A, the type initializes B once and shares it read-only across threads.

Equality geometry is independent of the objective

Cost weights, LM damping, and retraction metrics do not modify the equality basis. Bound factor views and binding-specific work are rebuilt for each point; mutable retraction workspaces are isolated for each call.

How do equality points enter CMC?

The explicit widening overload accepts ECMPoints and produces the concrete IE values used by the CMC graph, preserving numerical state and runtime equality-type identity. No inequality is removed. In the other direction, an explicit equality-view operation creates a new E type and point; ordinary upcasting never relaxes constraints.

Why does the concrete stored type matter?

Both point categories have their own dynamic-dimensional GTSAM traits. gtsam::Values stores concrete values: a stored ECMPoint must be extracted as ECMPoint, not IECMPoint. The shared substitution factor dispatches to the correct category; the base CMPoint is used by reference.

Can a manifold be implemented in C++?

Yes. The internal compiled adapters use gtsam::traits<T> for dimension, local coordinates, and retraction, plus explicit ambient recovery and Jacobian operations. IE adapters add boundary operations. No artificial constraint factors are required. GTSAM manifold compatibility alone does not make an unconstrained optimizer aware of corners.

What happens to fixed points and free variables?

Zero-dimensional points remain valid values and contribute fixed residual information without zero-width Gaussian variables. Free ambient variables retain their keys. Ownership and bindings are checked explicitly; component-point keys are separate from ambient keys, and temporal ordering is derived from ambient bindings.

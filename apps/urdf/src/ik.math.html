Transforms and residuals

Each pose X maps a link’s COM frame to the world. The model transform Fⱼ(qⱼ) maps the child COM frame into the parent COM frame, and C maps the tool frame into its carrier’s COM frame. The target pose T★ maps the desired tool frame to the world, with translation t★. The goal uses either a pose prior on the carrier pose X꜀ or a world-frame point residual.

\[ r_K=\operatorname{Log}\!\left(F_j(q_j)^{-1}X_p^{-1}X_c\right),\qquad r_T=\operatorname{Log}\!\left((T^\star C^{-1})^{-1}X_c\right),\qquad r_P=\operatorname{trans}(X_c C)-t^\star. \]

The fixed-link residual anchors a pose to its model rest transform X₀. Joint priors use the coordinate q₀ from the current configuration. The interval [a, b] comes from the URDF, and ε is the interior margin.

\[ r_B=\operatorname{Log}(X_0^{-1}X),\qquad r_Q=q_j-q_0,\qquad r_L=\max(0,a+\epsilon-q_j,q_j-b+\epsilon). \]

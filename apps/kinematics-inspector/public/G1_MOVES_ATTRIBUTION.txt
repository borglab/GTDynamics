Changes for this app: retained all 637 original rows (0 through 636), at the
native 60 Hz rate; normalized root quaternions and converted XYZW to WXYZ;
computed forward kinematics using the MJLab G1 model; inferred candidate
contact phases from sole height and horizontal corner speed; calculated and
recorded contact initialization, inverse kinematics, and ten CM-Opt smoothing
iterations. Render coordinates are quantized to 1e-6. Reference overlays show
the original motion, while initialization and smoothing views show our modified
estimates. No source motion frames were repeated or synthesized. The legacy IK
initializer uses a stationary commanded root rather than the recorded root path.

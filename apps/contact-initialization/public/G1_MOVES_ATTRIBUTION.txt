Changes made for this app: selected original rows 0 through 1799, a continuous
30-second window at the native 60 Hz rate; normalized root quaternions and
converted XYZW to WXYZ; computed pelvis-local G1 forward kinematics; inferred
candidate support episodes from world sole height and horizontal corner speed;
exported separate reference poses and stance diagnostics. No motion frames were
repeated, tiled, or synthesized. The app does not imply endorsement by the
creator, performer, capture provider, or Unitree Robotics.

The original 8-second MJLab policy sequence has separate provenance in
g1-walk.json and is not part of the G1 Moves dataset.
